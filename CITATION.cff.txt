abstract: >-
  An open, reproducible benchmark for measuring sanctions name-screening
  effectiveness against documented classes of name variation. Generates
  positive test cases by applying eighteen perturbation classes — spanning
  benign transliteration and naming-convention variation, adversarial Unicode
  evasion, and upstream system degradation — to names retrieved from published
  sanctions lists, together with synthetic negative controls. Reports a
  class-wise recall profile rather than a single accuracy figure, on the basis
  that the identity of a screening failure is more actionable than its
  magnitude.
